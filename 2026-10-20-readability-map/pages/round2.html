A noun with one variable loses its number

real canons: 174 of 234 statements change, and 255 variables are renamed
From canon's own canon
Now 
For every unit_1:
  when unit_1 is circular,
  checker reports a cycle for unit_1.

For every failing_cleanup_1:
  failing_cleanup_1 runs …
Proposed 
For every unit:
  when the unit is circular,
  checker reports a cycle for the unit.

For every failing cleanup:
  the failing cleanup runs …
Rule
Only where the statement holds one variable of that noun, and the noun appears nowhere else in it. Where it does, as in "a search that leaves one unit out and leaves out unit_1", the number stays: "the unit" could mean the "one unit" said just before. That kept the number in 60 statements, for 16 nouns.
Probes
Readers understood both versions equally well, and rated the proposed one a little easier in probe 2.
Risk
Low, with the rule above. "The unit" can only mean the one variable, because nothing else in the statement is a unit.
Lean
Change. It reads like prose rather than code, and it changes most statements, so the call is yours.
