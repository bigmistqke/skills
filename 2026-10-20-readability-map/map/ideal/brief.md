# Write the ideal prose

Each entry in your batch file is a statement from a software specification, generated by a tool from an Alloy 6 formula. The current words are exact (they lose nothing) but they read like a machine wrote them.

For each entry, write the prose that a careful human author of a specification would write for the same claim: as natural, short and plain as you can make it, while saying **exactly** what the formula says, no more and no less. Someone reading only your prose must be able to tell, for every possible situation, whether the claim holds.

Rules:
- Keep the domain phrases of the current words (such as "pulse has disposed", "is a spec", "cites"): they are the vocabulary's fixed readings. You may change their grammatical form (tense, "a hidden bug" for "a bug that is hidden", "it" for a repeated noun) where the meaning stays the same.
- Everything else is yours to change: order, sentence structure, lists or no lists, how variables are named, how quantifiers and conditions are said.
- Use lists where they help a reader, not by default.
- Alloy notes: `all`/`some`/`no`/`one`/`lone` quantify; `always`, `eventually`, `after`, `until`, `once`, `historically`, `before`, `since` are linear-time operators where "now" is the moment the claim is evaluated; `a until b` means b holds now or later, and a holds at every moment from now up to, not including, the first moment b holds. `a since b` means b held now or earlier, and a holds at every moment after the last such moment, up to and including now. `pred p[x: T] { … }` is a definition: "x is p exactly when …".
- If you cannot find natural prose that keeps the exact meaning, keep what you must and say so in `lost`.

Write your answers to `answers-K.json` in this folder (K is in your instructions), as a JSON array, one object per entry, in the batch order:
[{"id": "s012", "ideal": "…", "lost": "" , "note": "the one change that mattered most, in a few words"}]
`lost` is empty when your prose says exactly what the formula says; otherwise it names what your prose leaves out or adds.

Read only `brief.md` and your batch file. Do not read any other file.
