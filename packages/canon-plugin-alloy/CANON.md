# Canon: the Alloy plugin

How the Alloy plugin of the canon checker behaves now, and why. It is the canon of the package `@bigmistqke/canon-plugin-alloy`. It extends [the canon of the checker](../canon/CANON.md), whose terms it uses, and it writes its own terms and claims in Alloy, as the checker does. The plugin says the statements of a document from its formulas, and checks the claims of a canon against each other with the Alloy analyzer.

## Terms

### @term alloy-plugin

> The Alloy plugin is the plugin that writes the statements of a document from its formulas and checks its logic with the Alloy analyzer.

A document asks for it in its frontmatter. The commands `generate`, `check` and `logic` have a part in its work.

```alloy vocabulary

sig NoAnalyzer in Run {}
```

### @term generated-statement

> A generated statement is the statement of a unit that holds a formula, said from that formula between the markers of a generated region.

A person may write the statement instead, and the checker never overwrites it. A document keeps readings when it holds a table of them: only then does the checker say statements.

### @term word

> A word is a signature, a field of a signature or a predicate that a document declares in its model, and that its readings say.

A reading says a word in the language of the project. The words of a document are in order when three things hold. Each is declared in the term that names it. Its reading is beside its declaration. A claim, a definition or a fact uses it.

## @axiom the-claims-of-a-canon-never-contradict-each-other

<!-- statement-axiom-the-claims-of-a-canon-never-contradict-each-other:begin — generated by `canon generate`; edits are overwritten -->
> All of these hold:
> - for every [unit_1](../canon/CANON.md#term-unit):
>
>   when unit_1 is held with units that cannot all hold, [checker](../canon/CANON.md#term-checker) names unit_1 in its report of a contradiction

>

<!-- statement-axiom-the-claims-of-a-canon-never-contradict-each-other:end -->

Every claim of a canon says how the project is, and the claims are about one project. If two of them cannot both hold, the canon says nothing. A reader cannot follow both, and a change that obeys one breaks the other.

```alloy
(all u: Unit | u in Contradicting implies u in Named) and (all c: Scenario | c in Impossible implies c in Raised)
```

> - leaving out unit_1 lets scenario_1 happen
> - checker names unit_1 in its report of scenario_1
