# Canon: the Alloy plugin

How the Alloy plugin of the canon checker behaves now, and why. It is the canon of the package `@bigmistqke/canon-plugin-alloy`. It extends [the canon of the checker](../canon/CANON.md), whose terms it uses, and it writes its own terms and claims in Alloy, as the checker does. The plugin says the statements of a document from its formulas, and checks the claims of a canon against each other with the Alloy analyzer.

## @axiom a-formula-can-be-held-against-the-other-formulas

<!-- statement-axiom-a-formula-can-be-held-against-the-other-formulas:begin — generated by `canon generate`; edits are overwritten -->
> All of these hold:
> - for every [unit_1](../canon/CANON.md#term-unit):
>
>   when unit_1 holds a formula, is not a [term](../canon/CANON.md#term-term) and no other formula can contradict the formula of unit_1, [checker](../canon/CANON.md#term-checker) reports the formula of unit_1 as lone on a signature
> - for every unit_1 and every [word_1](#term-word):
>
>   when checker reports the formula of unit_1 as lone on word_1, no other formula can contradict the formula of unit_1.
<!-- statement-axiom-a-formula-can-be-held-against-the-other-formulas:end -->

A reader takes a unit with a formula as checked. The analyzer holds its formula against the other formulas, and would find a contradiction between them. That holds only when something else in the model speaks of the words the formula uses. A formula that no other formula can contradict holds whatever the rest of the canon says. It looks checked and is not, and the reader trusts a claim that nothing has checked.

So the checker reports a formula that no other formula can contradict, and only such a formula. It finds one by the signatures it is [lone](#term-lone-formula) on. The author can tie the signature to the rest of the model, with a definition, a fact or a scenario that names it. Or the author can take the formula away and write the statement of the unit by hand, so that the unit no longer looks checked. Then its test guards it.

```alloy
(all u: Unit | (u in Logical and u not in Term and u in Unchallenged) implies (some w: Signature | u->w in Expose.rel)) and (all u: Unit, w: Word | u->w in Expose.rel implies u in Unchallenged)
```

>
> There are [unit_1](../canon/CANON.md#term-unit) and signature_1 such that all of these hold:


## @axiom check-runs-wherever-node-runs

> Every check of `canon check` runs with Node alone. Only `canon logic` needs Java and the Alloy analyzer.

A project runs `canon check` on every change, in its continuous integration too. A check that needs Java and a jar of the analyzer would fail wherever they are missing, or the project would turn it off. So the checks of `check` read text, and the analyzer belongs to `logic`, which a project sets up on purpose.
